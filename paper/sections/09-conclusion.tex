\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We pre-registered a hypothesis about how residual-stream updates travel
through transformer depth, and the data answered with unusual precision:
structured, yes --- but the structure is substantially architectural where it
is predictive, pervasively depth-indexed where we hoped it was shared,
first-order and contractive where it is generative, distributed and
magnitude-driven where we tested it causally, and genuinely learned exactly
where the decomposition adds value over per-layer baselines. Both
pre-registered hypotheses did real work: H1 by surviving controls designed to
kill it, H2 by failing and thereby validating the unconstrained
parameterisation the field already uses. Every gate decision, threshold, and
fallback was written down in advance; every headline number carries a
confidence interval; and the full pipeline --- from cached activation tables
to every figure in this paper --- regenerates with one script. We offer the
results, the nulls, and the protocol in equal measure.
