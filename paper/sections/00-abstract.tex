\begin{abstract}
Transformer components communicate through an additive residual stream, so
the update a layer writes, $\Dl = v_{\ell+1} - \vl$, is the natural unit of
cross-layer computation. We pre-registered two hypotheses about these updates
on factual-recall inputs --- (H1) they are predictable across depth; (H2) the
prediction kernel is approximately depth-stationary --- and ran the frozen
protocol on \pythia{}, replicating on five further models from three families.
\textbf{(1)}~H1 holds far above pre-registered nulls but decomposes exactly:
the headline held-out $\Rsq = 0.65$ is $0.52$ running-sum tautology
$+\;0.13$ genuine structure, and the genuine part is substantially
architectural --- an untrained network shows it at comparable strength.
\textbf{(2)}~H2 fails decisively: kernels fit on one depth band predict
another band's updates worse than that band's mean, so the right abstraction
is a first-order ($\kstar\!\approx\!1$), fully depth-indexed state-space
model (SSM), validating the unconstrained parameterisation of cross-layer
transcoders. \textbf{(3)}~Fitted over frozen sparse sources, the SSM
reconstructs $\Dl$ above a matched per-layer dictionary ($+0.049$ $\Rsq$
rank-matched, $+0.008$ parameter-matched), with a depth-graded, roughly
half-learned gain; the learned state is contractive (median spectral radius
$0.43$, consistent with $\kstar\!\approx\!1$) and decodes the recalled
answer at mid-depth better than dimension-matched PCA of its own codes.
\textbf{(4)}~The pre-registered causal test returns a controlled null: no
single feature's ablation clears threshold, and the group effect that does is
reproduced by a norm-matched \emph{random direction} --- recall is a
distributed, magnitude-driven code. As a by-product, TopK dictionaries make
single-feature directions seed-unidentifiable where matched L1 dictionaries
on the same activations are perfectly stable. All gates were
frozen before data collection, every headline carries a paired bootstrap
confidence interval, and every figure regenerates from tracked tables.
\end{abstract}
