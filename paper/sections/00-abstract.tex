\begin{abstract}
Transformer components communicate through an additive residual stream, so the
update a layer writes, $\Dl = v_{\ell+1} - \vl$, is the natural unit of
cross-layer computation. We pre-registered a two-part hypothesis about these
updates on factual-recall inputs --- (H1) they are predictable across depth,
and (H2) the prediction kernel is approximately stationary in depth --- and ran
the frozen protocol to completion on \pythia{}, with replications on six models
from three architecture families (Pythia, GPT-2, Qwen2). Four findings survive
an adversarial battery of pre-registered nulls and post-hoc pressure tests.
\textbf{(1)}~H1 holds --- $\Dl$ is autoregressively predictable far above
scrambled and context-perturbation nulls --- but an exact variance
decomposition shows the raw score (held-out $\Rsq=0.65$) is part running-sum
tautology (0.52) and part genuine structure (0.13, rising to 0.44 when
refitted on the persistence-stripped stream), and an untrained-network control
shows the genuine part is \emph{substantially architectural rather than
learned}, on two families. \textbf{(2)}~H2 fails decisively: kernels fit on
one depth band predict another band's updates worse than that band's mean, so
depth dynamics are pervasively non-stationary, the effective AR order is
$\kstar\!\approx\!1$, and the right abstraction is a first-order, fully
depth-indexed state-space model (SSM) --- validating the unconstrained
per-layer-pair parameterisation of cross-layer transcoders. \textbf{(3)}~Fitted
over frozen sparse-autoencoder sources, the SSM reconstructs $\Dl$ above a
matched per-layer dictionary at every sparsity ($+0.049$
\CI{$+0.046$}{$+0.052$} $\Rsq$ at matched rank; $+0.008$
\CI{$+0.005$}{$+0.010$} at matched total parameters), with the gain growing
monotonically with depth within the Pythia family and replicating in sign in
GPT-2 and Qwen2; roughly half of it is already present at random
initialisation. The learned state is contractive at every layer
(spectral radius median $0.43$, memory $\approx0.8$ layers) --- the mechanism
behind $\kstar\!\approx\!1$ --- and is \emph{functionally informative}: a
32-dimensional state decodes the recalled answer at mid-depth better than
dimension-matched PCA summaries of the very codes it is built from.
\textbf{(4)}~The pre-registered causal existence proof returns a clean null:
no single feature's ablation moves the answer probability by the
pre-registered threshold, and the group-ablation effect that does is
magnitude-driven --- a norm-matched \emph{random direction} reproduces it ---
so factual recall is a distributed, mass-driven code with no privileged sparse
subset, a conclusion that survives swapping the dictionary. As a by-product we
isolate a practical failure mode: TopK dictionaries make single-feature
attributions seed-unidentifiable (decoder match $0.45$) where L1 dictionaries
on the same activations are perfectly stable ($1.00$). All gates, thresholds,
and fallback branches were written down before data collection; every headline
carries a paired bootstrap confidence interval; and every figure regenerates
from tracked tables with one script.
\end{abstract}
